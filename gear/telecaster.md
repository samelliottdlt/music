# Fender Standard Telecaster

Sam's Telecaster is a **Fender Standard Telecaster**, made in Mexico around
**2016**, in **Arctic White** with a **maple fretboard** (Sam's
description). A classic two-pickup solid-body electric.

## Controls (modern standard)

- 3-way blade: **toward bridge** = bridge pickup, **middle** = both,
  **toward neck** = neck pickup.
- Master volume, master tone. Some models have a 4-way switch (adds series).
  Vintage-spec reissues may wire positions differently — check the diagram.

## Using it with the rig

- Bridge pickup = bright twang; great with Chroma Console Reels (tape echo).
- Neck pickup + Space reverb = warm, mellow chords.
