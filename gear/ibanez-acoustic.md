# Ibanez TOD10N (Tim Henson signature)

Nylon-string acoustic-electric guitar, Tim Henson's signature model
([Ibanez artist page](https://www.ibanez.com/na/artists/detail/1172.html)).
It has a pickup and an onboard preamp with a tuner, so it plugs into the chain
like the electrics.

## Specs

From the [TOD10N spec page](https://www.ibanez.com/na/products/detail/tod10n_5b_02.html):

| | |
|---|---|
| Body | FRH hollow body with fan bracing; no soundhole on the top (less feedback), a sound port on the side |
| Woods | Solid Sitka spruce top, sapele back and sides, nyatoh neck, rosewood fretboard and bridge |
| Neck | 22 frets, 648 mm (25.5") scale, 46 mm nut; bone nut and saddle |
| Strings | D'Addario XTC45 (.028 .032 .040 .028W .035W .044W) |
| Tuning | Standard, E A D G B E (low to high) |
| Electronics | Fishman S-Core pickup, Ibanez AEQ210TF preamp with onboard tuner, 1/4in output, one 9 V battery |

## Preamp (AEQ210TF)

The controls run VOLUME, TUNER, TREBLE, PHASE, BASS from left to right, with
the neck side at the top (spec page). From the
[AEQ210TF manual p2](https://www.hoshinogakki.co.jp/pdf/ibanez/manual/en/ag/acos_guitar_preamp_AEQ210TF_EN.pdf#page=2):

- **VOLUME**: overall preamp gain. **TREBLE** / **BASS**: boost or cut.
- **PHASE**: reverses the phase to reduce feedback.
- **TUNER** button: tuner on/off. It doesn't switch itself off, so press it
  again when you're done. The note LEDs show A–G (plus # for sharps). The
  tuning guide shows a green LED in the middle when in tune, a red LED on
  the left when flat, and on the right when sharp.
- **Battery** (one 9 V): replace it when the sound gets quieter or worse, or
  when the tuner or its LEDs don't respond. Unplug the cable from the output
  jack when you're not playing so the battery isn't drained
  ([p3](https://www.hoshinogakki.co.jp/pdf/ibanez/manual/en/ag/acos_guitar_preamp_AEQ210TF_EN.pdf#page=3)).

## Using it with the rig

- 1/4in out → Chroma Console → amp, like the electrics (one instrument at a
  time), or straight into a free TX-6 channel (1/4in → 3.5mm cable, gain low).
- Feedback at higher volume: try the PHASE button first.
- Bypass heavy drive; nylon strings + Chroma Console Space/Reels/Cassette
  sounds great.

## Care

Stable temperature and humidity; avoid sun, cars, and radiators; wipe the
strings after playing.
