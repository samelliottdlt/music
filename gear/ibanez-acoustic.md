## Electronics (if acoustic-electric)

Many Ibanez acoustic-electrics (e.g. AEG series) have an under-saddle piezo
pickup, an onboard preamp with a built-in tuner, a 9V battery, and a 1/4in
output in the strap button. If yours has one:

- It's instrument level → plug into the pedalboard like the electrics, or
  directly into a free TX-6 channel (1/4in → 3.5mm cable, gain low).
- Bypass heavy drive; acoustic piezo + Chroma Console Space/Reels/Cassette
  sounds great.

If it has **no pickup**, record it with the OP-1 field's or OP-XY's built-in
mic (or a headset mic) — see `setup/recording.md`.

## Care

Stable temperature and humidity; avoid sun, cars, and radiators; wipe the
strings after playing.
