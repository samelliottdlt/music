## Controls (classic layout)

- **Upper-horn slide switch** picks the circuit:
  - **Rhythm (up):** neck pickup only, with its own volume + tone roller
    wheels. Darker, rounder.
  - **Lead (down):** lower plate controls — master volume + tone, plus three
    slide switches: neck on/off, bridge on/off, and **strangle** (low-cut
    that thins the tone; great for tightening fuzz).
- Set two tones in advance and flip between them with the circuit switch.
- Signature models (Johnny Marr, Kurt Cobain) differ — check the model page.

## Using it with the rig

- Strangle on + Chroma Console Fuzz = tight, cutting fuzz.
- Rhythm circuit + Space/Reels = dreamy, dark ambient chords.
